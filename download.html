Simple installation guide

1
Run the installer
Open CyberZen-Operations-Center-Setup-1.0.0.exe from your Downloads folder. If Windows SmartScreen appears, click More info and then Run anyway.
2
Add to Chrome
Extract cyberzen-chrome-extension.zip. In Chrome, open chrome://extensions, turn on Developer mode at the top right, click Load unpacked, and select the folder.
3
Ready to protect
CyberZen will start guarding your laptop immediately. When browsing, a subtle verification badge appears on visited websites to confirm they are safe.
Private and lightweight by design
CyberZen evaluates behavior on your local machine using lightweight machine learning models. Your files and personal history are never sent to external servers.
